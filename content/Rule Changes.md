There are major rule change to the way D&D works in OOTA2, mostly in the from lightly stealing from Daggerheart for better roleplay purposes.

# No Initiative
Players can now choose turn order themselves at the start of combat.

# Fear and Hope
The DM may randomly call on certain key roles as "Fear and Hope" rolls during critical moments.  When this occurs, the player rolls their d10 (Hope) against the DM's d10 (Fear).  

Generally speaking, a fear roll comes with consequences, while a Hope roll does not.  

> [!NOTE] Examples
>The player rolls a 5.  The DM rolls a 10. The Difficulty is 10.  The total is 15+Bonuses.  The roll is a "Successful with Fear".  The DM gains 1 Fear point.  Suddenly, you open the lock but it makes a loud sound, maybe alerting the guards.
>
>The player rolls a 5.  The DM rolls a 2.  The DC is 40.  The Total is 7+Bonuses.  The roll is a "Failure with Hope".  The player gains a hope point.  You fail to open the lock, but can try again.
>
>The player rolls a 1.  The DM rolls a 1.  On ANY duplicate, the player crits. No fear or hope is gained.  The lock opens instantly. 
>


## Hope Rules
Hope has a maximum of 6 per player.  It can be used in the following ways:

**Unlike fear, Hope can be used by any player at any time!**

1. Either Gain advantage or reroll you and only your dice roll (Including fear and hope rolls)
2. Take an extra action (No movement)
3. Help another player (Add d6 to another player's roll, does not apply to fear and hope rolls)
4. Restart your death saving throws
5. Reduce the effect of fear manipulation with a roleplay twist (Fear causes another goblin to appear, but he is stupid and slow)


## Fear Rules
Fear has a maximum of 10.  It will always be shown to the players.  It can be used in the following ways:

**Keep in mind that fear events can only occur after someone rolls FEAR**

1. Perform a spotlight to a monster.  This monster immediately moves and attacks
2. Change the environment. (It suddenly becomes foggy)
3. Advance Major Story events somewhere else in the game world
4. Create an enemy as backup
5. Cause mental changes
