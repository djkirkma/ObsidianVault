
### **Racing Mushroom Musume**
- **Stubborn:** slower but never stalls.
- **Reckless:** might sprint ahead or crash (losing a full turn).
- **Lucky:** gets advantage on one “burst” roll each race.  
    Players place bets, or magically “coach” a golem using a single reaction mid-race (shout a command, cast a harmless spell, etc.).  


### **Astral Spinners**
**Hook:** Three levitating star-orbs spin and align into constellations.  
**How it works:**  Each orb is a d6, but instead of numbers they show symbols:
- Star
- Moon
- Comet
- Sun
- Void
- Crown  
    You win based on symbol combos; Void triggers wild magic table-lite effects.
