The players are part of the inquisition, either being mercenaries or direct from [[Game World/Locations/Thetia]].  They are tasked with visiting the city of [[Game World/Locations/Hewe]] and investigation rumors of cultists that may be responsible for the year of shattered light.

After a day has passed, the players retire in an inn.  One of the players looks out the window to observe the stars.  They note that one star is heading right towards them.  After a crash, the star goes straight through the building an into the field.

Upon investigation, the players find an Onyx Gem.  The gem seems to be silent, but holds fraz-urb'luu.  They pose as a Deva, an emissary of a divine will.  They have been sent by the heavens to stop a great tragedy, but were intercepted and cast out of heaven.  They now ask for the help to free them by entering the dungeon.

The following day, the dungeon sprouts from the crash site.
