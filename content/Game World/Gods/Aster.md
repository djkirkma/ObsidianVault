## Character
Aster's character can be described as lawful evil.  He delights in seeing the death of living beings, especially those following the will of [[Game World/Gods/Serna]].  He famously can not lie, giving away this ability to [[Game World/Gods/Venith]], one of Aster's greatest weaknesses.
