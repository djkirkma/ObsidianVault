Aster is a member of the [[Founding Gods]], and are widely considered to be the God of Evil.

## Character
Aster's character can be described as lawful evil.  He delights in seeing the death of living beings, especially those following the will of [[Serna]].  He famously can not lie, giving away this ability to [[Venith]], one of Aster's greatest weaknesses.
