Aster is a member of the [[Game World/Gods/Founding Gods]], and are widely considered to be the God of Evil.  
## Character
Aster's character can be described as lawful evil.  He delights in seeing the death of living beings, especially those following the will of [[Game World/Gods/Serna]].  He has influence over lesser beings, and frequently uses minions such as devils, demons, and evil beings.   He famously can not lie, giving away this ability to [[Game World/Gods/Venith]].
