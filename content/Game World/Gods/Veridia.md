Veridia is a member of the [[Game World/Gods/Second Age Gods]].  Their followers represent scholars, truth, wisdom, and memory.

## Lore
Veridia was an elven mage assigned with the task with compiling a written recorded history for the elvish people during the [[Game World/Timelines/Varant/First Age]].  Veridia's initial chronicles were considered the paragon of elven historical scholarship. They were exhaustive, meticulously documented, and committed to absolute factual accuracy, forming the foundational texts of early elven memory.  In the modern era they are highly guarded secrets.

### Crisis
The integrity of Veridia's work was severely compromised following the ascension of [[Game World/Gods/Venith]]. This period was characterized by extreme political instability and systemic disinformation, which led to a catastrophic decline in the reliability of available sources. Veridia's records during this time reflect  the dizzying uncertainty of the age, rendering them academically problematic.  

Furthermore, historical records indicate that followers of Venith actively sought to control the historical narrative. They engaged in widespread acts of scholarly sabotage, including:
- The destruction of original manuscripts
- The modification or subtle corruption of existing texts
- The reproduction of forged histories 

Frustrated and driven by the necessity to safeguard truth from violation, Veridia embarked on a secret solution.  With their deep research, Veridia eventually discovered a new and different pathway to ascension that did not rely upon [[Game World/Gods/Founding Gods]]. They then utilized their ascension to transform their own mind and being into an eternal state of consciousness, which safeguarded against all attacks. 


## Veneration and Practice
Though instability caused by [[Game World/Gods/Venith]] has largely subsided in the modern era, the historical trauma of disinformation persists, leading to widespread scholarly superstition regarding the written word.  Consequently, Veridia's followers hold a ceremonial importance in modern society: high importance documents, such as historical books or binding royal decrees, are ritualistically written by trusted scholars devoted to Veridia. These writers pray for unerring accuracy, carrying on the tradition of hoping that devotion will guide their hands toward truth. Most notably, modern scholars universally practice small acts of textual warding; it is now an expected, almost superstitious, practice to stamp books, scrolls, and important charters with small devotional symbols or holy words dedicated to Veridia at the beginning or end of pages, serving as a metaphysical protective safeguard against the potential corruption of the text.
