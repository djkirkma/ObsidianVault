Venith is a member of the [[First Age Gods]].  They are the God of Tricks and Lies.  Generally speaking, they are the most powerful because of the abilities they took from the other gods.

## History
Venith is thought to have rose to Godhood on their own.  They were the final God created during the [[First Age]].  They believed that [[First Age Gods]] only brought pain and suffering, and wanted to return to a time were the only gods were the [[Founding Gods]].  They sought to destroy and kill all of the other [[First Age Gods]].  They especially despised [[Glyph]] and [[The God of Fear]].  

> [!info] Truth
> Venith was directly ascended to godhood with the help of all of the New Gods.  Venith in reality was deeply envious of their abilities, and lied to them that he would work to help each of them individually, without telling the others.  With the pieces of power he received from each of them, he was able to become a God behind their backs.  Once his folly was revealed, it was too late.
> 
> The founding gods helped him because they were worried about the rise of new godlike beings that could challenge them.  For example, Aster was greatly worried that Serna would kill him if a new god ever decided to help her.  Aster took a great risk in giving away his ability to lie, and the betrayal he received made him deeply angry.  However, he later realized this did pay off as Venith on his own stopped the ascent of new gods which in a roundabout way ended his worries.   
>  
> He does seek to stop all other new gods from being created, but only because he hates the idea of others influencing his own power.  He strives to live in a world where he is the only New God, and works to kill the others.  He successfully killed or misdirected all mortal beings seeking to become a new God, and no new Gods since of ever ascended. 
## Powers
Venith was granted a power from each of the old gods, each power of which they lost when they gave it away.  

The following are a list of their powers, each given away:
- Aster: To spin grand lies and deep confusion, even to other gods.
	- The first of his powers, used to lie to the other gods.  Nobody can ever truly know what Venith is thinking, his schemes twisting and turning.
- Serna: To influence the hearts of living beings for good or evil.
	- The most useful of his powers, it turns the heart of the second born prince against his brother for the throne.
- Terra: To cause events that would change the world physically.
	- Used to cause tragedies like earthquakes, or empower nations with good weather.  For what better twist of fate is that of the famine to the farmer?
- Entropy: To control the power of wild magic.
	- Frankly, Entropy got the best deal as the power of wild magic was something he thought Venith would be better than him at.  It helps him crackle the magic missile at the worst moment - or cause the fireball to go wide with too much power.  
- Fuera: Domain over the pure randomness of death.
	- Used this to change his own death to be never random. A twisted tool he uses, killing the best of heroes young and torturing those he hates with long life.
