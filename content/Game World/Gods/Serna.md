Serna is the God of Good, and is thought to be the enemy of [[Game World/Gods/Aster]]. 

## Character
Serna embodies the joy and purity of goodness as much as the fight against evil. She delights in acts of kindness, courage, and mercy, and is drawn to those who strive to make the world better. 

She is patient, nurturing, and encouraging toward those who wish to do good, inspiring hope and courage rather than fear. Her presence often uplifts mortals, reminding them of the beauty of light, the importance of moral action, and the strength found in kindness.
