Entropy is the God of Magic

## Character
Entropy is very difficult to characterize. To the layman, it is most accurately described as the embodiment of magic itself, though this description is necessarily limited.

What is known is that it controls magic, and they have been directly involved in moving the power of magic either to certain regions or to people.  

Very few have ever felt the direct attention of Entropy. Those who do often describe vertigo, a disorienting sense of the universe bending around them, or a feeling that reality itself has become unstable.  Mages struggle to record its behavior if any can be recorded at all, often leaving accounts filled with contradictions, uncertainties, and speculation.
