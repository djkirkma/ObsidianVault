While ascended aren’t gods in the primordial sense, ascended beings are still considered “gods” by most simply because they intervene most accurately like gods. These are mortals who gained god like power and they interfere in the world openly and frequently. To the average person, these are the gods you actually hear about, the ones who show up, cause trouble, change things, and leave marks on history. Over time, their constant open presence and the ambiguity of the [[Game World/Gods/Founding Gods]] led to this perception.  

## Achieving "Ascension"
Ascension generally require some small or large form of (but not all) of the following abilities:

- Immortality
- Control over magic
- Religious worship 
- Domanial command
- Matter manipulation
- Perception expansion
- Cognition enhancements
- Racial influence
- Body or form resilience
