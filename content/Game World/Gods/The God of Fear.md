The God of Fear[^1] is a member of the [[First Age Gods]] and responsible for the creation of the emotion of fear.

## History
The God of Fear an entity created sometime after [[Glyph]]'s rise to power.  They were influenced by [[Aster]] and sought to destroy all beings.

They used their power of fear to influence the minds of humans, leading to the first conflicts between men and other races during the rule of [[Glyph]].  

## Abilities
The God of Fear's abilities most often influenced the minds of mortal beings.  

[^1]: It is not a mistake that the name of the God of Fear is unwritten, it is unknown by scholars at this time.
