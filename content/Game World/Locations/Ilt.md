Ilt is an oligarchic elvish nation to the west of the game world.

## Closed Council
The ruling body of government in Ilt is a closed council of 10 elvish faction leaders.  It is mostly comprised of elvish nobility.  

## Culture
Ilt is predominantly full elvish citizens with some small percentage of half-elf races.   Non-elvish races are rarely allowed into the country.  Slavery is outlawed.  

## Wisdom Dominance
The wisdom of the high elves is the country's greatest asset.  They have the largest and oldest collection of scholars in the world, with large libraries and highly trained scholars.  While other countries might scramble at threats to their nation, the elves are able to swift and effectively deal with problems by finding complex solutions.
