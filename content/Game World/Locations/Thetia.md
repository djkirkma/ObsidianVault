Thetia is a human feudal monarchy in the center of the world.  In the [[Game World/Timelines/Varant/Year of Shattered Light]], it's king is his holiness King Juve.  The capitol city is [[Enine]].  

## History


## Culture
Thetia is a primary proponent to the fight between humans and other races.  While they do allow other races into it's territory, they are banned from holding any form of government.  However, it is not uncommon to see other races in the nation as Thetia employs a certain "lawful slavery" in the form of peasant workers.  It would not be uncommon to see a human baron with a town partially consisting of dwarven serfs.


## Holy Inquisition
One of the predominant parts of Thetia's government, the Holy Inquisition is the religious body of Thetia.  The Inquisition advises the king and controls the churches and root out heresy in the nation.  They are lead by the [[Eminence]].

One of the ways that they achieve this goal is with inquisitors.  They are holy investigators, and have been known to torture or blackmail those who do not submit to the wills of the church.

## Feudal
