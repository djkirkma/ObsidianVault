Thame was a great human city in the nation of the [[Game World/Locations/Old Empire]].  

## Government 
Thame was ruled by a council of important members of the city (Lords, Religious Leaders, Heads of commerce, etc.).  The members were appointed by council vote, and the council made all decisions about matters.

## Manufacturing
Thame was very wealthy from it's manufacturing of great tools that it sold beyond it's borders.  These tools touched all styles of lives - from amulets that could increase a merchant's influence, to the lowly blacksmith's hammer that was harder than steel.  The way in which they produced these goods was a close guarded secret.  Later, these factories were repurposed into creating great weapons of war - legends say of great swords that could cut bone, and bows that had the force of boulders.
