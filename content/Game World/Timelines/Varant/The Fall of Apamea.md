Recorded documents from [Apamea] detail some invasion during Year 400.  Rather than fall into ruin, documents suggest that a golden age began after this invasion with great strides in the creation of food and clean water.   At some point after this invasion, the name of the city was changed from Apamea to [Thame].  For clarity sake, scholars now refer to the city from year 400 and onwards as Thame, though original records go back and forth on this for some time during the transition until later years.
