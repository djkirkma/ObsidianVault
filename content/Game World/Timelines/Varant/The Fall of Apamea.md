[Apamea] fell to a small invasion during Year 400.  Rather than fall into ruin, documents suggest that the great golden age of Apamea began after the end of this invasion with great strides in creation of tools for farming and the herding of cattle and sheep.

At some point after this invasion, the name of the city was changed from Apamea to [Thame].  For clarity sake, scholars now refer to the city from year 400 and onwards as Thame, though original records go back and forth on this for some time during the transition until later years.
