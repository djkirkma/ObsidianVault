Gracklstugh is a major duergar city located in the Underdark. It is a heavily fortified, industrial metropolis carved into massive caverns, known for its extensive forges, mines, and ironworks. The city is organized with strict hierarchy and strict laws, reflecting the duergar’s disciplined and often harsh society. Its streets and tunnels are crowded with workshops, markets, and barracks, and it is defended by both trained militias and magical wards. Gracklstugh is a hub of trade, particularly in iron, weapons, and other crafted goods, and its inhabitants are known for their skill in mining, smithing, and subterranean craftsmanship.
