Elyjah was a monk of dubious origin and a former member of [[Characters/The Five]].  He was a known loner and did not speak much of his past or how he found himself in the Underdark.  He had a disagreement with The Five in the city of [[Blingdenstone]] and fought to stop them, but was defeated. 

# Abilities
Those in The Five described Elyjah's abilities drawing from his Ki along with the tattoos he had on his body.  He had a proficiency with fists and fought with martial arts.  Known tattoos include:

- Spider
- Sun
- Tree
- Phoenix
