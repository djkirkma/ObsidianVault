Jepos was an Forest Gnome Assassin and a member of [[Characters/The Five]].  Most notably, Jepos' origins are unclear, as he frequently stated that he had issues with amnesia.   Witness testimony from [[Characters/Kaios (Reyor Gaerfroy)]] imply him to be some some dubious criminal origin.

## Abilities
He was known to have carried a Vampiric Dagger of some magical origin.
