Reyor Gaefroy ("Kaios") was a Wild Mage Tiefling from the Dukedom of Gaerfroy in the world of [[Game World/Faerun]] and was firstborn male of the Gaerfroy royal family.  He had a younger human brother Tyfi.  He was a member of [[Characters/The Five]]. 

## History
Kaios is one of the most well documented members of the five because of his history with the dwarves of [[Gauntlgrym]].   

Born as the heir of the Dukedom of Gaefroy, he has a background of being in a position of status. His reason for leaving his home is debated - though the dwarves point the most likely reason being his infernal nature or rather some dispute between him and his brother for inheritance.  At 13 years old, he spent some years on the roads of Faerun, doing odd jobs and earning coin before wandering into the Sword Coast North and working with dwarves more frequently.

Kaios had some noted involvement with the Drow excursion of Gauntylgrym and was a known friend of [[Bruenor Battlehammer]].  The dwarven records note him as heroic, but he departed the dwarves sometime later.  He was later captured by the Drow and tortured in [[Velkenvelve]] where he met [[Characters/The Five]] (and other members, notably [[John Guthog]] and [[Elyjah ]]).  He escaped and visited the cities of [[Blingdenstone]], [[Game World/Gracklstugh]], [[Neverlight Grove]], and [[Sloobludop]] while attempting to return to the surface.  Upon the return of the five to the surface, he carried news of personal sightings of demonic entities outside the norm even for the Underdark (Notably: [[Demogorgon]]).    Later (at request of the king) he and the The Five met with various factions and began a quest to return back to the Underdark to investigate further demonic incursions.  


## Abilities
Kaios had spellcasting abilities very young, and drew upon infernal ancestry.  However, this magic was uncontrolled and often lead to unintended effects.  Others within his party noted frequent uses of the spells Sleep, Darkness, and Magic Missile.  He spoke Dwarvish.
