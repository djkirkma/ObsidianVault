Draxiros Klerjoth was an Eldritch Knight Dragonborn from the city of Tipa in the Forgotten Realms.  He worked with the shephard royal family and was trained by a dwarf named Balrik and a wizard named Alma.  He had a squire named Razam. He was commonly known as Drax.

Drax is one of the five heroes tasked with returning to the underdark before the events of the [[Game World/Timelines/Varant/Year of Shattered Light]].  They carried two myconids: [[Characters/Stool]], and [[Characters/Rumpadump]] along with a sword known as [[Characters/Dawnbringer]].
