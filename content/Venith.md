Venith is a member of the [[First Age Gods]].  They are the God of Tricks and Lies.


## History
Venith is thought to have rose to Godhood on their own.  They were the final God created during the [[First Age]].  They believed that [[First Age Gods]] only brought pain and suffering, and wanted to return to a time were the only gods were the [[Founding Gods]].  They sought to destroy and kill all of the other [[First Age Gods]].  They especially despised [[Glyph]] and [[The God of Fear]].  

> [!info] Truth
> Venith was directly ascended to godhood with the help of all of the New Gods.  Venith in reality was deeply envious of their abilities, and lied to them that he would work to help each of them individually, without telling the others.  With the pieces of power he stole or learned from each of them, he was able to become a God behind their backs.  Once his folly was revealed, it was too late.
> 
> He does seek to stop all other new gods from being created, but only because he hates the idea of others influencing his own power.  He strives to live in a world where he is the only New God, and works to kill the others.  He successfully killed or misdirected all mortal beings seeking to become a new God, and no new Gods since of ever ascended.
