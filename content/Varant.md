# Magic System

#### Glyphs
Magic is far more difficult for lower born races and humans.  However, humans in particular have direct access to the glyphic system[^2].  These are inscribed runes upon items that imbue them with magical powers.  

#### Make magic magical again
Unlike [[Game World/Locations/Faerun]], magic casters are not very common.  While anyone can use glyphs, the ability to cast a spell requires both the innate magical ability and the correct vocal components.   

#### Verbal Magic 
Varant's magic casting system is more verbal than other magic systems.  For enhanced roleplay purposes, I ask that for spells you create your own verbal components and stick with them for the campaign.  The higher tier spell, the more grandiose.  

The following is an example of magic missile, 1st level:
> "Triad of light, unerring flight!"

Or cast at a higher level:
> "Triad of light, unerring flight!"
> "Swift as thought, strike as true!"

The following is an example of chain lighting, 6th level:
> “O roaring titan who strides upon stormclouds,
Heir of thunder, lord of the split sky—
I summon thee from the trembling vaults above.
Gather in my grasp the serpents of the tempest,
Coil and strike with the fury of divine judgment!
Let the heavens rend, let sinners know thy name,
And let all who stand before me be marked by thy roar!
***-Chain Lightning!***

#### Magic Tiers

| Category     | % of Population      | D&D Magic Equivalent |
| ------------ | -------------------- | -------------------- |
| Beginner     | 1%                   | Cantrips             |
| Intermediate | 0.5%                 | 1-3                  |
| Advanced     | 0.1%                 | 4-5                  |
| Venerated    | 0.01%  (1 in 10,000) | 5-6                  |
| Saint        | 0.05% (1 in 5,000)   | 6-7                  |
| Seraphim     | ~100 confirmed users | 7-8                  |
| Nephilim     | No recorded cases    | 9                    |

#### Physical Tiers
Physical abilities do not have a verbal requirement, but do still follow the tier system.  An example of a Saint tier ability would be (Level 13: Deflect Energy).  

Generally, people are referred to as their name and their rank at Venerated and beyond.[^1]
